## Option Comparison

The safest current decision is to keep release `NO-GO` and make the blocker
decision-ready. Waiting for upstream proof or explicitly deferring the blocker
requires no dependency changes. Compatibility exception is possible only with
release-owner approval and must not be described as remediation. Replacement,
removal, or a reviewed Next.js pin/change are separate dependency or demo-scope
PRs and need their own audit proof.
