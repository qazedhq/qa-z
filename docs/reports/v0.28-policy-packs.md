# v0.28 - Rules And Policy Packs

Date: 2026-05-28

Status: local product slice complete.

## Goal

Introduce a minimal local policy-pack surface that lets teams validate merge
policy inputs and run guard with an explicit policy name.

## Change

- Added `qa-z policy validate`.
- Added builtin `default` and `strict` policy packs.
- Added configured `merge_policy` validation from `qa-z.yaml`.
- Added `qa-z guard --policy strict` policy validation and verdict metadata.
- Documented policy usage and local-only boundaries.

## Validation

- `tests/test_policy_command.py::test_policy_validate_accepts_builtin_strict_pack`
  failed first because `policy` was not registered, then passed.
- `tests/test_policy_command.py::test_policy_validate_rejects_empty_configured_block_on`
  covers invalid configured policy input.
- `tests/test_policy_command.py::test_guard_accepts_strict_policy_and_records_it`
  covers `qa-z guard --policy strict` and verdict metadata.
- `tests/test_public_docs_current_truth.py::test_policy_docs_pin_local_only_boundaries`
  pins policy docs and local-only boundaries.

## Explicit Non-Actions

- No policy pack contacts external services.
- No target-repository repair is applied.
- No GitHub comments, uploads, tags, releases, deployments, or credential access
  were added.
