## Brand Account vs Builder Account Strategy

Use `@qazedhq` as the durable project account:

- post release notes, demos, safety notes, and launch-week updates;
- reply sparingly when QA-Z directly adds useful context;
- maintain lists for AI coding agents, devtools, AppSec/QA, and AI engineering;
- keep the voice calm, technical, and evidence-first.

Use builder or maintainer accounts for personal commentary:

- share behind-the-scenes implementation notes;
- ask for feedback from existing relationships;
- avoid making the brand account look like a bot or engagement farm.

The brand account should be the canonical project voice. Builder accounts can
amplify manually, but they should not simulate adoption or coordinate spam.
